# Backlog

Items the factory has not built yet. `/factory next` takes the first
unchecked item and starts a job for it. Replace these with your own.

These three starter items are the ones from the video
(https://youtu.be/ctoaIC4LHmI?t=1012s). The video's factory checked the
repo, found none of them existed yet, and built them through the loop.
